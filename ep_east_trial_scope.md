# EcoSyncField — Field Trial Scope
**Eyre Peninsula East Landscape Board · Mapping Services Australia**

Trial commences: Tuesday ____________   ·   Review: ____________ (6 weeks)

---

## Purpose

To evaluate EcoSyncField in routine weed control operations, with particular attention to
the three things the Board has identified as mattering most:

1. **Data flow** — getting records into the Board's GIS, in the Board's own structure
2. **Positional accuracy** — verified, not claimed
3. **Simplicity** — can a field officer use it without training, and without remembering to

---

## What MSA provides

- One EcoSyncField unit, charger, trigger pad and mount
- Configuration to the Board's requirements
- A hosted feature layer built to the Board's schema, shared with the Board
- On-site demonstration and a field session on day one
- Firmware updates and support for the duration of the trial
- A written summary at the review

## What the Board provides

- A nominated field officer to use the unit in normal work
- A nominated GIS contact
- **The feature class definition** — field names, types, coded-value domains (especially the
  species list)
- **Confirmation of datum** — GDA94 or GDA2020
- Written feedback at the review

---

## What gets measured

| | |
|---|---|
| **Records captured** | Count, across how many field days |
| **Positional accuracy** | Every record stores its own accuracy value. Verified on day one against a known mark and against a phone at the same position. |
| **Data flow** | Did records reach the Board's GIS, in the Board's schema, and how much effort did it take? |
| **Operator experience** | Time to learn. Anything missed or forgotten. Anything that got in the way of the actual work. |
| **Reliability** | Device restarts, data loss events, battery performance over a working day |

---

## Success criteria

Agreed up front so the trial has a clear answer at the end.

- [ ] **500+ records** captured over **5+ field days**
- [ ] **95%+ of records within 3 m** by their own recorded accuracy value
- [ ] Records visible in the Board's GIS, **in the Board's schema**, within 24 hours of a
      field day
- [ ] The field officer can operate the unit unaided after a single demonstration
- [ ] **No unrecoverable data loss**

---

## Known limitations — stated up front

- **There is no power switch yet.** The unit runs continuously and must be **charged
  nightly**. A power switch is in development.
- Accuracy is **1.5 – 1.7 m typical**, not centimetre. No correction subscription is used or
  required.
- The unit is hand-built. Two exist. This is early-stage equipment being trialled as such.

---

## Out of scope for this trial

- EcoSyncMicro and EcoSyncID — both in development, neither available
- RTK or centimetre-level accuracy
- Writing directly into the Board's ArcGIS Enterprise system
